\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\textit{Route engines and learned residuals.} OSRM is an open-source routing engine over OpenStreetMap data~\cite{luxen2011real}; route engines estimate travel time from road-class speed profiles and know nothing of hub dwell or a corridor's history. DeeprETA~\cite{hu2022deepreta} post-processes a route planner's estimate with a learned residual at industrial scale, the structure we use; we differ in making localisation of the planner's error primary and in reporting by slice. Other learned estimators address urban trajectories~\cite{wang2018learning,wang2018deeptte,derrowpinion2021eta}; delivery-time prediction is surveyed in a preprint~\cite{wen2023survey}, and boosting is a strong baseline for it~\cite{khiari2020boosting}.

\textit{Freight reliability.} Transport research characterises corridors with descriptive indices such as the planning-time and buffer indices~\cite{fhwa2005reliability,jones2005measuring,margiotta2013shrp2}; we share the unit of analysis and add multiplicity-controlled testing that respects dependence.

\textit{Inference.} False discovery rate control~\cite{benjamini1995controlling}, large-scale inference~\cite{efron2010large}, its validity under dependence~\cite{benjamini2001control}, permutation tests~\cite{phipson2010permutation} and cluster-robust inference~\cite{cameron2015practitioner} frame the audit; empirical-Bayes ranking~\cite{efron1975data,morris1983parametric,gelman2007data} frames the shrinkage; the Diebold--Mariano comparison~\cite{diebold1995comparing,harvey1997testing}, rolling-origin evaluation~\cite{tashman2000out,bergmeir2012use} and the leakage literature~\cite{kaufman2012leakage,kapoor2023leakage} frame the evaluation.

\textit{On this dataset.} Searches in September and October 2026 found notebooks and case studies on the Delhivery records, typically comparing actual and OSRM time, but no peer-reviewed study; this is a statement about the search, not about the world. Every result is reported beside a trivial policy computed on the same data.
