## 0 · A finding the owner must see first: D-052 targets a networking conference

D-052 names "ICCCI 2027 (20 February 2027)". The venue it actually read is the **9th
International Conference on Computer Communication and the Internet** (Tokyo, 25-27 June
2027; `iccci.org`; submission 20 February 2027; 4-10 pages; double-blind). Its topic list is
communication networks, IoT and services, multimedia communications, network management,
security, and network architecture. **Nothing in it covers freight analytics, statistical audit
of a routing engine, or LLM agents.**

The conference the work order means, **ICCCI = International Conference on Computational
Collective Intelligence** (Springer LNAI/CCIS, organised from Wroclaw), is a different event
that shares the acronym and nothing else. Its 2026 edition (18th) ran 23-25 September 2026 in
Heraklion; its **2027 call does not exist yet** (searched `iccci.pwr.edu.pl`, EasyChair and
general web search on 21 October 2026). D-052's own update of 14 October records the same:
"the ICCCI 2027 call for papers is not yet published".

Honest consequence: the 20 February 2027 date in the project plan belongs to the networking
conference, whose scope fit is 1 of 5. The paper should not be written to that date or to
that scope. Decisions D-095 and D-097 record this; D-052 itself is not edited.

**Extended-conference-paper rule.** The work order assumes a journal version needs "at least
30% new material". **No journal on this list was found to state that percentage on a page
the tool could read.** A blog says IEEE T-ITS wants a conference precursor disclosed and kept
under 40% overlap; no IEEE source confirmed it. Springer Nature, Elsevier and IEEE all ask for
disclosure of the earlier version and a substantial difference; the exact percentage is the
editor's call. **Action: read the "prior publication / extended conference paper" paragraph of
the chosen journal's guide for authors on the day of submission and record the number in
`docs/decisions.md`.** Keep the 30% as the project's own floor regardless.

## 3 · Recommendation (a recommendation: the owner decides)

**Conference: ECML PKDD 2027, Applied Data Science track** - provided its call opens as the
previous cycle did (abstract and paper in March). Reasons: the track is written for exactly
this paper shape (real data, sound method, honest evaluation; novelty "not required"),
the 16-page LNCS limit matches the conference manuscript planned in the paper structure
decision, review is double-blind, reproducibility is encouraged (the repository's strongest
property), and the stated acceptance rate (about 24%) is realistic. Risks: the 2027 call is
unannounced, so a March 2027 deadline is an inference from one previous cycle; a Springer
LNCS paper needs one author to register and present in person.
**Fallback: ICCCI (Computational Collective Intelligence) 2027**, same LNCS template, 12-15
pages, if its call appears; fit is 3, not 4, because the paper is not about collective
intelligence. **Not recommended: the Tokyo ICCCI** that D-052 targeted (fit 1).

**Journal: Journal of Big Data (Springer Nature)** for the extended version. Reasons: scope
covers analytics platforms, applications and case studies, so the streaming section, the
scale appendix and the reproducibility package count as contributions rather than padding; the
journal states a median of 47 days to a first decision; open access matches the project's
own open-code, open-data stance. Risks: the APC (about USD 1,990, third-party figure) must be
paid or waived; indexing and the current impact factor must be read on the publisher page.
**Stretch alternative: Transportation Research Part E**, which fits the freight-network
subject better but will demand operational insight and external validity this data cannot
supply (one operator, 22 days). A free subscription route exists at Elsevier hybrid journals
(Part E, Part C, ESWA) if the APC is a blocker.

**Preprint first.** Post the arXiv preprint before submission (ECML PKDD 2026 and DSAA allow
this; the DSAA page asks for a month's lead). Do not post it if the chosen venue's call says
otherwise; read that line on the day.
